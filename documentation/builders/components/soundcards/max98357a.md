# MAX98357A (I²S amplifier)

The MAX98357A is a small I²S amplifier for one speaker. It needs no driver beyond the device tree overlay.

## Setup

Choose `max98357a` as the sound card in the web app (Settings → Plugins → Raspberry Pi → Sound card), then run
`lauschctl setup raspi` on the box and reboot. The step adds `dtoverlay=max98357a` to `/boot/firmware/config.txt`
and switches the on-chip audio off.

## Wiring

| MAX98357A | Raspberry Pi GPIO | Physical pin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VIN | 5V | 2 or 4 |
| GND | Ground | 6 |
| BCLK | GPIO18 (PCM_CLK) | 12 |
| LRC | GPIO19 (PCM_FS) | 35 |
| DIN | GPIO21 (PCM_DOUT) | 40 |

`GAIN` and `SD` can stay unconnected (default gain, amplifier always on).

## Notes

* The board has no volume control of its own. Lauschkiste regulates the volume in software; if the box is too
  quiet or too loud at the same setting, change `volume.mixer` and the output's `volume_limit` (see
  [Audio](../../audio.md)).
* A soft click when the amplifier starts can occur with this board; Lauschkiste starts with a short silence, fades in the first samples and
  ends with silence to keep it small.
