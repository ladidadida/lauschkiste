# Your first card

This page needs an RFID reader that works with the box (see [RFID readers and cards](../hardware/readers.md); a USB
reader is the easy start) and something to play: music you uploaded, a radio station or a podcast, see
[The box is running](first-steps.md).

## Put it on a card

1. Let the album, station or podcast play and tap the **card button** in the player.
2. Hold a card on the reader.
3. The card is now registered. Take it away and put it back: it starts again.

A new card the box does not know yet shows **Register** in the player. All cards are listed under
Settings → Cards.

## Hand it over

Give the card to your child. That is the whole idea: card on, music plays.

Further cards work the same way. Besides albums, a card can start a podcast, a radio station, an audiobook, or do things
such as *louder*, *pause* or *shut down*: see [Cards](../using/cards.md).
