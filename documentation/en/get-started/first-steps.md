# Your first card

The box is running. Now it gets music and a card.

## 1. Put music on the box

1. Open the web page of the box: `http://<name>.local`.
2. Open **Library**, then **Music** and the **Folders** view.
3. Choose **Upload** and pick a folder with an album from your computer. One folder per album.

Audiobooks go the same way: Library → **Audiobooks** → Folders → Upload, one folder per book. The files of a book are
its chapters and play in the order of their names (`2.mp3` comes before `10.mp3`).

<img src="../../assets/screenshots/en/upload.png" alt="Library, Music, Folders with the Upload button" width="260">

The library notices the new files by itself after a few seconds. More about it in [Music and the library](../using/music.md).

## 2. Play it

Tap the album or book. The player on the **Start** page shows what plays. Tap play, change the volume with the slider.
If you hear nothing, check that the speakers are on and the volume is up; then see
[Sound and Bluetooth](../using/sound.md).

<img src="../../assets/screenshots/en/player.png" alt="The player with the card button" width="260">

## 3. Put it on a card

1. While the album plays, tap the **card button** in the player.
2. Hold a card on the reader.
3. The card is now registered. Take it away and put it back: the album starts.

A new card the box does not know yet shows **Register** in the player. All cards are listed under
Settings → Cards.

## 4. Hand it over

Give the card to your child. That is the whole idea: card on, music plays.

Further cards work the same way. Besides albums, a card can start a podcast, a radio station, an audiobook, or do things
such as *louder*, *pause* or *shut down*: see [Cards](../using/cards.md).
