# Radio

Internet radio stations have a name, an address and optionally a picture. Each card plays one station.

## Add stations

In the library open **Radio** and tap **Add station**:

- **Search.** With the plugin *Radio directories* (Settings → Plugins) the dialog searches
  [radio-browser.info](https://www.radio-browser.info), an open directory. Type a name or a topic such as "kinder";
  stations are listed with logo, country and bitrate. Without a search term, the popular stations of your country
  appear. One tap adds a station. What you type is sent to radio-browser.info.
- **By address.** *Add by address* takes the address of the stream. It may also be an `.m3u` or `.pls` file from the
  station's website; the box finds the stream in it.

The menu next to the button imports and exports lists of stations as `.m3u` files, the format that radio apps use.

## On a card

Play the station and tap the card button in the player, or choose *Play radio* when registering a card in the card
list.

## While it plays

The player shows the station's picture and name, and what the station says about itself: the current title, as far as
it is sent. A stream that drops out is reconnected by itself.

Stations you listened to last are listed under Library → **Continue**. Its menu can remove an entry from that list (the
station stays). The same list holds audiobooks and podcasts.
