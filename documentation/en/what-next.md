# What next?

The box plays. Here is what you can add, sorted by what you want.

## I want more to listen to

- **Audiobooks** that continue where the child stopped: [Audiobooks](using/audiobooks.md). Own server with books?
  [Audiobookshelf](using/audiobooks.md#audiobookshelf) connects it.
- **Podcasts** with search and subscribing: [Podcasts](using/podcasts.md).
- **Radio stations** from the internet: [Radio](using/radio.md).
- **Cover pictures and playlists** for the library: [Music and the library](using/music.md).

## I want to control what is played

- **Quiet hours and a daily time**: [Time limits](using/time-limits.md). The box is silent at night and stops after
  the agreed time.
- **A card for the grown-ups** that adds extra time, shuts the box down or changes the volume: [Cards](using/cards.md).

## I want it to sound better or be more convenient

- **Better speakers or a sound card**: [Sound cards and amplifiers](hardware/sound-cards.md).
- **Bluetooth headphones**: [Sound and Bluetooth](using/sound.md).
- **Buttons and knobs on the box** for volume, next and pause: [Buttons and knobs](hardware/buttons.md).
- **A power button** and a battery for a box that goes along: [Power button and battery](hardware/power.md).
- **A screen** that shows the player: [A screen on the box](hardware/screen.md).

## I want to take it along

- **The box opens its own WiFi** when there is none, so a phone can still reach it:
  [Away from home](using/wifi-hotspot.md).

## I want to copy music the easy way

- **Upload in the web page** (works from a phone too) or a **network folder** in the file manager of your computer:
  [Music and the library](using/music.md#getting-files-onto-the-box).

## Looking after the box

- [Updating](maintenance/update-uninstall.md) when a new version is out.
- [When something does not work](maintenance/troubleshooting.md).
