# Player

The player adapts to what is playing. Tapping the title opens it in the library.

## Controls

| Content | Buttons | Shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Music | shuffle, previous, play/pause, next, repeat | title, artist, album |
| Audiobook, podcast | back 30 s, previous chapter, play/pause, next chapter, forward 30 s | chapter x of y |
| Radio | play/pause | station and current title |

Audiobooks and podcast episodes remember where they stopped and go back a few seconds when they continue (Settings → Playback & audio). Chapters always play in order, regardless of shuffle and repeat.

## The bar below the buttons

- **Search** (magnifier): find music by title, artist or album. Tapping plays it, the arrow opens the album.
- **Up next** or **Chapters**: the queue; tap an entry to jump to it.
- **Speed** (e.g. 1×): for audiobooks and podcasts, 0.75× to 1.5×.
- **Put on a card** (card button): put what is playing on the next card placed on the reader. A card that is already in use doesn't start playing, and you are asked before it is overwritten.
- **Sleep timer** (moon): stops after 15 to 90 minutes, optionally fading out, or at the end of the song, chapter or episode. While a timer runs, its remaining time is shown; tap it again to switch it off.

## Unknown card

When a card that doesn't play anything yet is placed on the reader, the player shows a notice with "Register", with what is playing preselected.
